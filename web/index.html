How to read the evidence
TLS and cipher suite come from the endpoint's handshake records. Browser errors alone cannot distinguish unsupported TLS from a DNS, certificate, or service problem.
ECH accepted means the server accepted encrypted ClientHello. An ECH-shaped GREASE extension is not proof of acceptance. The stale case needs its initial connection and subsequent recovery examined together.
Client IP is observed by the server. This local lab correctly reports loopback addresses. Raw logs are intended for the local study, separate from any public comparison.
